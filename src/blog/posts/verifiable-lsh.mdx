## Motivation

Nearest neighbor search is everywhere.
Proving you did it _correctly_ without revealing the dataset is not.

## High-level idea

We combine:

- LSH for bucketing
- Verifiable inner products
- A ZK circuit enforcing correct selection

## What went wrong

- Quantization is harder than it looks
- Halo2 layouts punish bad abstractions
- Borrow checker pain is real

## Open questions

- Can we reduce constraints further?
- Is there a better hash family for this setting?
